# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - First release
- Anvil upgrade system from +1 to +10 for weapons, tools, bows, crossbows, shields and armor, using any Smithing Template and XP.
- Success chance 95% at +1 down to 50% at +10; a failed upgrade drops the item one level.
- Bonuses per level: +6% Attack Damage (weapons, tools), +2% mining speed (pickaxes), +6% arrow damage (bows, crossbows),
  +0.3 Armor and Toughness (shields), +0.3 Armor and +0.2 Toughness (armor), +10% max durability (all).
- Optional support for Spell Power Attribute, Wizards and Bard (RPG Series).
- Colored names by level, animated +9 / +10 names, a +10 color scheme for each item type.
- Star Seal effects in the world and in the anvil screen, Lunar Choir sounds, Easy Anvils support.
- Commands `/lunaticupgrader set|get`.
